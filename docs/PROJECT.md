# Project

Demo of six navigation layers on one ERP screen. In Concept the active tab is the only source of truth. Current keeps today's behaviour. Live at https://erp-navigation-concept.vercel.app. Names and figures in the demo are made up.

## Done

- Current and Concept on one screen. Concept follows the active tab. Current can leave the sidebar behind it.
- Amber mark on the tab and the sidebar when they point at different places. Concept never shows it.
- The inner sidebar eases open and closed with the active tab. Reduced motion snaps it.
- A new tab grows from zero width. A closed tab shrinks, and the neighbours slide into the gap. Reduced motion skips the animation.
